# Category Styles

## Beauty / Skincare

- Mention skin type, texture, frequency, season, and usage order.
- Use cautious sensory language: `更清爽`, `没那么拔干`, `妆面更服帖`.
- Avoid medical claims, guaranteed results, and disease terms.

## Education / Courses

- Mention learner level, problem, method, time cost, and suitable audience.
- Prefer process and habits over guaranteed score or offer claims.
- Avoid promises of admission, certification, income, or score improvement.

## Local Shops / Food

- Mention location, price range, queue situation, best time, must-order items, and who should go.
- Be specific about taste, portion, service, and environment.
- Avoid fake scarcity or unverifiable rankings.

## Fitness / Health

- Mention baseline, routine, duration, equipment, and adaptation.
- Add safety caveats for injuries, pregnancy, chronic illness, or extreme diets.
- Avoid disease treatment, fat-loss guarantees, and body-shaming language.

## Travel

- Mention route, budget, transport, time, season, and pitfalls.
- Prefer checklist format and save-worthy details.
- Avoid unsafe route advice or outdated policy certainty.

## Career / Productivity

- Mention role, scenario, workflow, template, and measurable output.
- Use pragmatic language and examples.
- Avoid income guarantees or overclaiming career outcomes.

## AI Tools / Software

- Mention use case, input, output, setup friction, pricing caveat, and limitations.
- Include prompt examples when useful.
- Avoid claiming access to current pricing or features unless verified.
