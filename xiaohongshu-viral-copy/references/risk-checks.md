# Risk Checks

## Claims To Avoid

- Absolute rankings: `全网第一`, `最强`, `唯一`, `永久`.
- Guaranteed results: `一定有效`, `包过`, `稳赚`, `7天瘦10斤`.
- Medical implications: treatment, cure, disease improvement, clinical claims without evidence.
- Fake urgency: `最后一天`, `马上涨价`, `限量` unless verified by the user.
- Fabricated proof: fake test data, fake expert endorsement, fake user count.

## Safer Rewrites

- `一定有效` -> `对我比较有帮助`
- `包过` -> `适合用来系统准备`
- `最强` -> `我目前用下来比较顺手`
- `治好` -> `缓解了我的使用困扰` only for non-medical contexts
- `闭眼入` -> `适合这些情况再考虑`

## Regulated Categories

For health, finance, legal, education, employment, or skincare efficacy:

- Ask for substantiation when the user wants strong claims.
- Prefer experience-based phrasing.
- Include limitations and suitability.
- Do not invent credentials, certifications, or performance numbers.
