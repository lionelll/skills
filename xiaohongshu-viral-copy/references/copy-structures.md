# Copy Structures

## Full Note Structure

Use this default structure for a complete Xiaohongshu note:

1. Hook: relatable pain, surprising result, direct conclusion, or conflict.
2. Context: who this is for and why the author cares.
3. Details: 3-5 concrete points with scene, usage, contrast, or steps.
4. Proof: observation, before/after feeling, checklist, or limitation.
5. Soft CTA: invite comments, saves, or questions.
6. Tags: 3-6 precise SEO tags plus broader discovery tags when useful.

## Input Brief Checklist

Use this checklist to gather or infer enough material:

- Product/topic name.
- Category and note type.
- Audience: age/life stage, problem, budget, experience level.
- Core details: price, specs, features, ingredients, location, route, method, or deliverables.
- Proof: user experience, comparison, case, before/after observation, usage duration, limitation.
- Scene: commute, office, study, travel, home, holiday, gifting, urgent problem, beginner use.
- CTA: save, comment, private message, store visit, purchase, consultation, template download.
- Constraints: forbidden terms, brand tone, legal/compliance boundaries.

If some fields are unknown, proceed with assumptions unless the missing field changes the factual claim or risk level.

## Note Type Selection

- Product review: detailed single-product evaluation.
- Tutorial: steps, checklist, beginner guide, process walkthrough.
- Knowledge explainer: concept, ingredient, method, policy, or common misconception.
- Pain-point solution: one clear user problem and a practical route out.
- Comparison review: two or more options, decision criteria, who should choose what.
- Collection/list: multiple recommendations grouped by scene, budget, or user type.
- Experience story: personal journey, mistake, turning point, result, reflection.
- Local visit: shop, restaurant, exhibition, hotel, city walk, service experience.
- Case study: before/after, problem, action, outcome, what to copy.
- Trend commentary: hot topic, new feature, new style, seasonal opportunity.

## Seeding / Conversion

Use when the goal is product recommendation or purchase interest.

Structure:

1. Pain point or scene.
2. Discovery moment.
3. Benefits tied to concrete usage.
4. Limitation or who it is not for.
5. Soft CTA.

Avoid sounding like a direct ad. Mention tradeoffs when possible.

## Experience Sharing

Use when the user wants a personal, educational, or IP-building post.

Structure:

1. Identity or situation.
2. Mistake, turning point, or new realization.
3. Method summary.
4. Steps or checklist.
5. Result or reflection.

## Review / Avoid Pitfalls

Use when the user asks for 测评, 避坑, 值不值得, or comparison.

Structure:

1. Direct conclusion.
2. Who it suits and who it does not suit.
3. Pros and cons.
4. Real usage details.
5. Decision advice.

## Rewrite Rules

- Preserve the user's facts and intent.
- Improve the hook before changing the rest.
- Replace abstract claims with scenes, numbers, or examples.
- Remove stiff brand language.
- Keep paragraphs short enough to scan on mobile.
