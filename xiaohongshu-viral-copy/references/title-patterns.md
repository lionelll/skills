# Title Patterns

Use 5-10 title candidates when the user asks for title optimization. Keep most titles within 20 Chinese characters when possible. Use emojis only when they sharpen the click cue.

## Angle Mix

- Pain point: `为什么你总是...`
- Result: `坚持7天后，我发现...`
- Curiosity: `这个方法没人告诉我...`
- Anti-common-sense: `我劝你先别急着...`
- Crowd-specific: `新手/打工人/学生党/宝妈看过来`
- List-based: `我整理了5个...`
- Contrast: `以前...现在...`
- Avoidance: `别再这样...了`
- Low-threshold: `不用...也能...`
- Direct conclusion: `适合...，不适合...`

## High-Click Formulas

- `不是...而是...`
- `普通人也能用的...`
- `我试了...终于明白...`
- `花了...才总结出的...`
- `如果你也...先看这篇`
- `被问爆的...整理好了`
- `...前一定要知道的事`
- `...真的有用吗？实测说话`

## Title Quality Rules

- Make one promise only. Do not pack three benefits into one title.
- Put the most searchable noun in the title when SEO matters.
- Prefer concrete audience or scene over vague adjectives.
- Avoid absolute ranking claims such as `全网第一`, `最强`, `唯一`.
- Avoid medical, financial, or education guarantees.

## Optimization Pass

When optimizing an existing title:

1. Preserve the factual subject.
2. Add one of: audience, result, conflict, time cost, price cue, or scene.
3. Remove generic adjectives before adding slang.
4. Generate at least one restrained title and one high-energy title so the user can choose tone.
