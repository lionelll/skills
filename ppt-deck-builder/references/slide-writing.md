# Slide Writing

## Slide Plan Fields

For each slide, define:

- Slide number.
- Title.
- One-sentence message.
- Layout type.
- Visible content.
- Visual or chart idea.
- Speaker notes.

## Title Rules

Use assertion titles when possible:

- Weak: `Market Overview`
- Strong: `The market is shifting toward AI-native workflows`

Use label titles for section dividers, appendix pages, and training modules.

## Bullet Rules

- Use 3-5 bullets per content slide.
- Keep bullets parallel in grammar and length.
- Start with the most important point.
- Replace generic words with concrete nouns and verbs.
- Avoid stacking sub-bullets unless the slide is a deliberate checklist.

## Speaker Notes

Use notes for:

- Narration.
- Evidence and caveats.
- Source context.
- Transitions.
- Timing.
- Discussion prompts.

Do not put long paragraphs on the slide just because they matter.

## Common Slide Patterns

Problem slide:

```text
Title: [Audience] faces [specific pain] at [moment]
Content:
- Current behavior
- Friction or cost
- Consequence
Visual: user journey, before/after, or pain-point map
```

Recommendation slide:

```text
Title: Prioritize [action] to achieve [outcome]
Content:
- Action 1
- Action 2
- Action 3
Visual: roadmap, decision matrix, or priority stack
```

Comparison slide:

```text
Title: Option B offers the best balance of [criteria]
Content:
- Criteria rows
- Option columns
- Recommendation highlight
Visual: table or 2x2 matrix
```

Data insight slide:

```text
Title: [Metric] changed by [amount], driven by [reason]
Content:
- Insight
- Driver
- Implication
Visual: line, bar, waterfall, funnel, or cohort chart
```
