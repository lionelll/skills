# Deck Types

## Business Report

Recommended sequence:

1. Cover
2. Executive summary
3. Context / objective
4. Key findings
5. Data evidence
6. Implications
7. Recommendations
8. Timeline / next steps
9. Risks and mitigations
10. Closing

Best for quarterly updates, strategy reviews, project status, and management reports.

## Investor / Fundraising Pitch

Recommended sequence:

1. Cover with clear company/category signal
2. Problem
3. Solution
4. Why now
5. Product
6. Market
7. Business model
8. Traction
9. Go-to-market
10. Competition
11. Team
12. Financials / ask
13. Closing

Keep claims evidence-backed. Put detailed assumptions in notes or appendix.

## Product Launch

Recommended sequence:

1. Product name and launch promise
2. Target user and pain
3. Product overview
4. Core features
5. Use cases
6. Differentiation
7. Demo flow
8. Pricing / packaging if relevant
9. Launch plan
10. Call to action

## Training / Courseware

Recommended sequence:

1. Learning objective
2. Agenda
3. Concept introduction
4. Example
5. Step-by-step method
6. Practice / exercise
7. Common mistakes
8. Summary checklist
9. Further resources

Make each section teach one concept and include practice prompts.

## Academic / Research Talk

Recommended sequence:

1. Title and authors
2. Research question
3. Motivation
4. Background
5. Method
6. Dataset / experiment
7. Results
8. Discussion
9. Limitations
10. Conclusion

Use figures and equations only when they help the audience follow the argument.

## Roadshow / Video Deck

Recommended sequence:

1. Hook
2. Problem
3. Opportunity
4. Solution
5. Proof
6. Use cases
7. Business / impact
8. Roadmap
9. Call to action

Design for narration: fewer words, larger visual moments, clear transitions.
